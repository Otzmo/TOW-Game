          Keyboard shortcuts

          Controls

          • Drag a unit: move it. If its front ends within 1″ of an enemy edge, it is aligned to it for the charge.
          • Drag the gold corners: wheel. White circle: pivot around the centre. Shift: 15° steps.
          • Skirmishers: drag a model to move it alone, Shift + drag moves the whole unit.
          • Drag a character onto a friendly unit to join it.
          • Measure / Line of Sight: choose the tool and drag on the table. Esc returns to Select.
          • Mouse wheel: zoom. Drag empty table: pan the view (when zoomed in).
          • Setup: follow the steps in Battlefield ▸, then Start Battle.
          • All keyboard shortcuts: Shortcuts on the left, or ? / F1.
